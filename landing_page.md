# Hardcore Fitness 101 Landing Page Plan

## 1. Strategy overview
- **Main promise and emotional hook:** A friendly Agoura Hills and Westlake Village community that gives busy adults the energy, strength, and consistency they have been missing through coaching, accountability, and supportive group training.
- **Primary call to action:** Schedule a quick tour and chat with a coach.
- **Secondary call to action:** Book your first class or request a quick text to answer questions if you prefer to ease in.
- **Main objections to overcome:** Worries about time, feeling out of shape, nervous about starting, and past attempts that felt unsupported or impersonal.

## 2. Section by section wireframe

1. **Hero section**  
   - **Goal:** Grab attention, communicate the promise, and drive immediate action.  
   - **Includes:** Headline, subheadline, short bullets on support and community, social proof line, primary CTA button, secondary CTA text link.  
   - **Visuals:** Warm photo of women in class, badge for Agoura Hills and Westlake Village, small review stars or short quote overlay.

2. **Who this is for**  
   - **Goal:** Show busy women and local professionals that this page speaks to them.  
   - **Includes:** Short intro sentence, bullets on common life scenarios and goals.  
   - **Visuals:** Icons for calendar, kids, laptop, heart/health, light supportive background block.

3. **What makes Hardcore Fitness 101 different**  
   - **Goal:** Differentiate from big box gyms and boutique spots that felt impersonal.  
   - **Includes:** Three to five pillars on community coaching, accountability, flexible options, upgraded facility, and beginner friendly vibe.  
   - **Visuals:** Two-column layout with photos of the facility zones (Redzone Rumble, outdoor patio, infrared sauna), checkmarks.

4. **How it works**  
   - **Goal:** Make the process simple from first click to feeling consistent.  
   - **Includes:** Three-step timeline: schedule, visit and plan, feel supported each week.  
   - **Visuals:** Numbered steps, minimal icons, connective line or arrows.

5. **Social proof**  
   - **Goal:** Let local moms and professionals speak to trust, safety, and results.  
   - **Includes:** Three short testimonial quotes, brief first name and neighborhood.  
   - **Visuals:** Quote cards, subtle accent color, possible star icons.

6. **Objection handling**  
   - **Goal:** Address busy schedule, out of shape, nervous to start, tried everything.  
   - **Includes:** Short reassurance paragraphs with permission-based language and CTA reminder.  
   - **Visuals:** Accordion or stacked cards with icons.

7. **Call to action**  
   - **Goal:** Make the next step clear and low pressure.  
   - **Includes:** Headline, brief reassurance, main CTA button, secondary text option to request a text.  
   - **Visuals:** Highlighted background, centered content.

8. **FAQ**  
   - **Goal:** Remove remaining friction on comfort, safety, beginner friendliness, scheduling, and day one expectations.  
   - **Includes:** Question and short answer list with supportive tone.  
   - **Visuals:** Accordion style list, calm background.

## 3. Full written copy of all sections

### Hero section
**Headline:** Small group training that feels like a supportive neighborhood crew

**Subheadline:** If you are a busy Agoura Hills or Westlake Village adult who wants more energy and confidence, come see how our coaches make consistency feel doable.

**Bullets:**
- Coaching, accountability, and flexible nutrition guidance that fits real life
- Strength, conditioning, and cardio boxing classes that meet you where you are
- Upgraded space with Redzone Rumble room, infrared sauna, and outdoor training patio

**Social proof line:** Trusted by local moms and professionals who wanted guidance not a crowded scene.

**Primary CTA button:** Schedule a quick tour and chat with a coach

**Secondary text link:** Would you be open to booking your first class instead

### Who this is for
Busy women and local professionals who want structure without pressure.

**You might relate if:**
- You juggle work, kids, and a packed calendar but still want to feel strong and energized
- You prefer being coached and encouraged instead of wandering a big gym alone
- You want accountability, nutrition guidance, and a community that notices when you show up
- You like the idea of a clean, refreshed space with parking that is easy in and out

### What makes Hardcore Fitness 101 different
- Community and coaching first so you feel seen and supported every visit
- Small class sizes with Lift Camp, Booty Camp, and Fight Camp cardio boxing to keep things fun
- Beginner friendly approach that still challenges you to progress each week
- Flexible membership options and simple scheduling for busy Agoura and Westlake lives
- New equipment, infrared lighting, sauna access, and an outdoor patio that feels like a reset

### How it works
1. Schedule a quick tour and chat with a coach so we can learn your goals and schedule
2. Visit the club, see the Redzone Rumble room and training patio, and map out your plan together
3. Start with your first class and feel supported with coaching, accountability, and InBody scans to track progress

### Social proof
- “I was nervous to start again after two kids. The coaches checked in every week and now I have the energy to keep up with soccer runs.” – Sara from Agoura Hills
- “I tried the big gyms and never felt noticed. Here they greeted me by name and even remembered my schedule.” – Jen who works in Westlake Village
- “The Redzone Rumble classes are my stress relief after work. I feel stronger and finally consistent.” – Marisa local business owner

### Objection handling
**I am too busy:** Would you be open to letting us build a schedule that matches your work and family calendar We have morning, mid day, and evening options plus quick check ins by text.

**I am out of shape:** Do you mind me asking what pace feels right for you Our coaches give beginner friendly modifications and keep an eye on you so you never feel lost.

**I am nervous to start:** You can come in for a quick tour first and see the space. We will introduce you to the coach on duty so your first class feels familiar.

**I have tried everything:** Let us keep it simple with coaching, accountability, and InBody tracking. We will celebrate small wins and adjust together instead of leaving you on your own.

### Call to action
Ready to see if Hardcore Fitness 101 feels like your place

Schedule a quick tour and chat with a coach

Prefer to jump in Book your first class and we will greet you at the door.

### FAQ
**What should I expect on day one**
Arrive ten minutes early so we can say hi, show you around, and set you up for class.

**Do I need to be in shape before starting**
Not at all. We meet you where you are with form checks and options in every class.

**How do I fit this into my schedule**
We help you pick class times that match work and family blocks and we can adjust weekly if needed.

**Is it comfortable for beginners**
Yes. Classes are small, coaches are hands on, and you will see other adults juggling the same life demands.

**What should I wear and bring**
Comfortable workout clothes, sneakers, and a water bottle. We have cubbies, a getting ready area, and free parking.

**What if I want nutrition support**
We keep it flexible with simple guidance and optional check ins. We also offer InBody scans to track progress.
